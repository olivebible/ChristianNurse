How it works

  1. Register

    Fill in the form below with the patient's name, your village or town and the care needed.

  2. Mother calls you back

    She talks with you, understands the need and confirms whether a visit is possible near you.

  3. Care begins at home

    A visit is fixed at a time that suits the family, with your doctor's advice followed throughout.

About Mother Ramanamma Garu

మదర్ రామనమ్మ గారు

Mother Ramanamma Garu is a retired nurse of the Government of Andhra Pradesh. Her years in government service taught her how to care for people calmly, cleanly and with respect, whether they are young, old, rich or poor.

She now serves as a Christian nurse. Her care is practical first: checks, dressings, guidance and follow-up. Where a family wishes, she also prays with them and reads from Scripture. Patients of every background are welcome, and prayer is never pressed on anyone.
